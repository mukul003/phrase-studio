Title: Work Life — Learn and Practise
Phrase repetitions: 1
Example repetitions: 1
Intro: Some days, you sit down at your desk and the questions start straight away. What time are you free? Can you finish this today? Let’s find a few simple words for those moments.

Sentence 1: I start work at nine.
Summary: Someone asks about your working day. Just give them your usual starting time. A simple sentence is enough.
Example: A colleague asks when you’ll be online. You could say, “I start work at eight, so I’m usually ready for a call by half past.”
Cue: Say that as if someone had just asked when you start.

Sentence 2: Could you help me with this?
Summary: You’ve looked at the same problem for ten minutes. A colleague might spot something you’ve missed.
Example: You turn to someone on your team and say, “Could you help me with this report? I’m not sure about the last part.”
Cue: Say that as if your teammate were sitting beside you.
Repeat phrase: 2

Outro: You don’t need a long speech to start a conversation at work. A starting time, a small question—that’s enough to get going. Try one of these sentences today. See you next time!
